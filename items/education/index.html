Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

No single body ranks these 20 together; each item's inclusion is grounded in real data from the sources below, but the overall list and order is editorial. Items 1–12 map directly to UNESCO's ISCED 2011 levels and UIS-tracked indicators. Education technology infrastructure (17) and Special needs & inclusive education (18) are both tracked under UN SDG indicator 4.a.1 (UNESCO UIS as custodian agency). Standardized testing & credentialing (19) is tracked by the OECD's PISA program. Language & bilingual education (20) is tracked under UN SDG indicator 4.5.2 (UNESCO UIS). Online learning/EdTech, educational publishing, student financing/loans, and corporate training (13–16) have no institution that tracks the sector itself and remain editorial.
Item citation: UNESCO Institute for Statistics, ISCED 2011 · UN SDG Indicator 4.a.1 · OECD PISA Data Explorer · UNESCO UIS Data Browser (SDG 4.5.2)
