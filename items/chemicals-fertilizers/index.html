No single body ranks these 20 together; each item's inclusion is grounded in real data from the sources below, but the overall list and order is editorial. Fertilizer items (1–5) are directly tracked by IFA/IFASTAT. Plastics & polymers (7) is tracked by the OECD's Global Plastics Outlook / Plastics Use dataset. Pesticides & agrochemicals (8) is tracked by FAO's FAOSTAT Pesticides Use dataset. Rubber (13) is tracked by the International Rubber Study Group (IRSG), an intergovernmental body whose mission is to publish natural and synthetic rubber market statistics. The remaining industrial chemicals (6, 9–12, 14–20) have no single institutional ranking source and remain editorial.
