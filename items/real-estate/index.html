Nareit's live REIT market data is broken out by its 14-sector property classification (Office, Industrial, Retail, Lodging/Resorts, Residential, Timberland, Health Care, Self-Storage, Telecommunications, Data Center, and more), directly sourcing REITs (5) plus 8 property-type items by REIT sub-sector: Commercial property/office (2), Industrial & logistics (3), Retail real estate (4), Hospitality (8), Data center (9), Multifamily & apartment (11), Healthcare & medical (14), Self-storage (15), Digital infrastructure/cell tower (17). OECD's Housing Prices Database tracks Residential property (1); FAOSTAT's Land Use domain tracks Agricultural land (7); FAO's Global Forest Resources Assessment tracks Forestry & timberland (19); the OECD Affordable Housing Database tracks Affordable housing (20); the Federal Reserve's Financial Accounts (Z.1) and Mortgage Bankers Association survey data track Mortgage & real estate finance (12); the US Census Bureau's New Residential Construction release tracks Multifamily & apartment housing (11) alongside Nareit. UN-Habitat/World Bank, as SDG indicator 1.4.2 custodians, track secure land tenure — sourcing Land value & ownership (6), though that covers tenure specifically, not market pricing. UN-Habitat, as SDG 11.3.1 custodian, tracks the land-consumption-to-population-growth ratio, sourcing Urban land use policy (13). Property development, Mixed-use development, and Vacation & second-home property have no verified institutional tracker and stay editorial.
