Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

17 of 20 items are institutionally tracked, upgraded from an all-judgment baseline. Several reuse sources already verified elsewhere for the same physical product: Automobiles & motor vehicles (1) via OICA, Semiconductor manufacturing (4) via WSTS/SIA, Basic metals & steel (6) via the World Steel Association, Petroleum refining (7) via EIA's oil refining capacity data, and Industrial robotics & automation (9) via the International Federation of Robotics' World Robotics report. The remaining 12 upgrades use World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade HS-code trade data: Machinery & heavy equipment (3, HS 84), Aerospace manufacturing (5, HS 8802), Consumer appliances (10, HS 8509), Fabricated metal products (11, HS 73), Electrical equipment (12, HS 85), Construction equipment (13, HS 8429), Rail & locomotive manufacturing (14, HS 8602), Precision & scientific instruments (16, HS 90), Packaging machinery (17, HS 8422), Furniture manufacturing (18, HS 94), Agricultural machinery (19, HS 8433), and Printing & publishing equipment (20, HS 8443) — trade-flow (export) statistics, not domestic production rankings, a narrower match than the item names imply. Electronics assembly (2), Shipbuilding (8), and Advanced & additive manufacturing (15) remain editorial — no HS code or institutional tracker cleanly matches the assembly activity, shipbuilding output, or additive-manufacturing equipment specifically; UNIDO's ranked industrial statistics remained unreachable.
Item citations: UN Statistics Division, ISIC Rev.4 · OICA · WSTS · World Steel Association · EIA · International Federation of Robotics, World Robotics report · World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade (HS 84, 8802, 8509, 73, 85, 8429, 8602, 90, 8422, 94, 8433, 8443)
