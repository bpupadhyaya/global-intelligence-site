Shipbuilding

Editorial judgment Checked against IMO GISIS and UNCTAD's Review of Maritime Transport; neither could be confirmed as a working comparative production tracker this pass. Included here on domain-knowledge judgment, not fabricated data.

Shipbuilding sits within Manufacturing & Industrial Goods, the sector converting raw materials into everything usable — the core measure of national economic capacity — a category with a real, cited scale: 14.89% of world GDP in manufacturing value added, 2024 (World Bank).
