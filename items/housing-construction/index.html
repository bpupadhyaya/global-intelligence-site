A universal need for shelter, and one of the largest employers and GDP contributors worldwide.

Adjacent Industry value added, including construction, is 24.98% of world GDP (2025) — this indicator bundles construction together with mining, manufacturing, and utilities, not construction alone. World Bank

Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

16 of 20 items are institutionally tracked. The U.S. Census Bureau's Value of Construction Put in Place survey sources Residential construction (1), Home renovation & remodeling (18), Commercial real estate development (4), and Urban infrastructure (9, via its public-works spending categories) — all US-specific, narrower than the item names imply. USGS's Mineral Commodity Summaries covers Cement (2), Concrete & aggregates (3), and half of Drywall, siding & building finishing (20, gypsum wallboard specifically). World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade HS-code trade data sources Structural steel (6), Construction equipment (8), Glass & insulation (11), HVAC systems (16), and Prefab & modular construction (14). FAO Forest Products Statistics sources Timber & lumber (7); the Federal Reserve's Mortgage Debt Outstanding series sources Mortgages & housing finance (5); the OECD Affordable Housing Database sources Affordable housing (13); and GlobalABC/UNEP's Global Status Report for Buildings and Construction sources Green & sustainable building (17). Plumbing & electrical (MEP) systems (10), Roofing (12), Real estate brokerage & services (15), and Architecture & engineering services (19) remain editorial — no single tracked category cleanly matches each.
