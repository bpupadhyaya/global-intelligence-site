💧 Water

More essential than any traded good — days without it, not weeks — yet the least marketized item on this list. Scarcity is a rising geopolitical flashpoint.

Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

14 of 20 upgraded via aggressive institutional search. Items 1–4 share one FAO AQUASTAT page covering total renewable water resources and the 69%/12%/19% agricultural/municipal/industrial withdrawal split. Items 5–6 (drinking water, sanitation) are the WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme's core global indicator. Item 7 (wastewater) and item 11 (water stress) are UN Stats SDG 6 indicators, cross-verified against FAO AQUASTAT's wastewater module. Item 8 (dams) is ICOLD's World Register of Dams; item 9 (hydropower) is the International Hydropower Association's World Hydropower Outlook; item 10 (desalination) is tracked as a non-conventional water source in AQUASTAT's main database. Item 12's $1.04T figure is the same World Bank water page used for the category-scale citation. Item 15 (flood & drought) is UNDRR's DesInventar disaster-loss database. Item 17 (transboundary basins) is UN Stats SDG indicator 6.5.2. Six items stay judgment: 13 (employment), 14 (groundwater management), 16 (bottled water), 18 (stormwater management), 19 (virtual water), and 20 (water pricing) — no institutional tracker was confirmed live for any of these.
