Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

No single body ranks these 20 together; each item's inclusion is grounded in real data from the sources below, but the overall list and order is editorial. CIRFS confirms man-made fibers are ~74% of global fiber production by volume (#1) and separately tracks polyamide/nylon (#10) and cellulosics/viscose-rayon (#14) by fibre type. USDA FAS's Cotton: World Markets and Trade circular tracks Cotton (#2); IWTO's Market Information report tracks Wool (#7); APICCAPS's World Footwear Yearbook tracks Footwear (#4); EDANA's Global Nonwoven Markets Report tracks Non-woven textiles (#13); World Bank/UNCTAD/WTO WITS (UN Comtrade) HS 6309 "worn clothing" trade data tracks Secondhand & resale apparel (#20). The rest has no single ranking source and is editorial.
