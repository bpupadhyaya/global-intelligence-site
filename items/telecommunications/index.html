Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

No single body ranks these 20 together; each item's inclusion is grounded in real data from the sources below, but the overall list and order is editorial. Items 1–10 are directly tracked ITU indicators. Undersea cables (11, carrying ~99% of intercontinental data traffic) is sourced to the International Cable Protection Committee (ICPC), a genuine intergovernmental/industry body (253 members across 71 countries) that publishes its own aggregate submarine-cable-network statistics (~500 cable systems, ~1.8M km); note ICPC defers to TeleGeography, a private research firm, for cable-by-cable detail, so the citation covers the aggregate figure only. Equipment/CDN/IoT/data-center/tower/handset/ emergency-comms/private-5G items (12–20) remain fully editorial.
