Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

10 of 20 items are institutionally tracked. No single cross-modal ranking authority exists; maritime, aviation, and auto items (1–5) remain sourced via UNCTADstat, IATA Economics, and OICA. The International Union of Railways' RAILISA platform (100+ railway companies' freight tonne-kilometres since 1995) sources Rail freight (7). IMF PortWatch, reused from Supply chain & trade routes, sources Ports & terminals (8). The EIA's natural gas pipeline data sources Pipelines (9) — US-specific, narrower than the item name implies. World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade HS 8708 trade-flow data sources Automotive parts & components supply chain (17). ICAO's Air Transport Monthly Monitor and GIS Aviation Data source Airport infrastructure (19) — air-traffic and geographic aviation data, not construction/capacity investment specifically. Trucking, Public transit, Electric vehicles, Warehousing & distribution, Last-mile delivery, Freight forwarding, Ride-hailing & micromobility, Cold chain & refrigerated logistics, Shipbuilding, and Logistics & fleet-management technology remain editorial — IEA's Global EV Outlook and the International Transport Forum were both fully bot-blocked (403) on every URL tried, and no verified tracker exists for the rest.
