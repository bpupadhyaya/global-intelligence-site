15 of 20 upgraded, mostly via the BIS statistics portal, the single richest source for this category. Banking & lending (1) via BIS Locational/Consolidated Banking Statistics; Government bonds (2) via BIS Debt Securities Statistics' central-government table; FX (5) via the BIS Triennial Central Bank Survey ($7.5T/day); Derivatives (6) via BIS OTC Derivatives Statistics; Payments infrastructure (10) via the BIS CPMI "Red Book" country payment-system reports; Central banking (11) via BIS Central Bank Policy Rates. Equity markets (3) via the World Bank's market-capitalization indicator. Insurance (7) via the IAIS Global Insurance Market Report. Asset management (9), Hedge funds (14), and Securitization/ABS (16) via the FSB's Global Monitoring Report on Non-Bank Financial Intermediation. Sovereign wealth funds (12) via IFSWF, the official international forum owned by SWFs themselves. Credit rating agencies (18) via IOSCO's dedicated CRA-oversight publications. Cryptocurrency (19) via the FSB's crypto-asset monitoring work. Remittances (20) via World Bank/KNOMAD. Five items stay judgment: 4 (corporate bonds), 8 (pension funds), 13 (private equity & venture capital), 15 (reinsurance), and 17 (repo markets) — no institutional tracker was confirmed live for any of these.
