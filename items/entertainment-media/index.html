Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

IFPI confirms real, current 2026 music-industry figures ($31.7B global recorded music revenue, #6) and separately tracks streaming as its own measured revenue segment (streaming surpassed $22B, 69.6% of recorded-music income), sourcing Streaming audio platforms (#18) too. WIPO's Global Innovation Index tracks national feature-film production counts across 104 economies, sourcing Film & streaming (#1) — covers film production output specifically, not streaming subscriber/revenue figures. The AECOM/TEA Theme Index tracks global theme park & museum attendance, sourcing Theme parks & tourism entertainment (#10). The International Publishers Association's International Publishing Data report tracks publishing-market data across 76 countries, sourcing Publishing (#8). The Reuters Institute's Digital News Report tracks news consumption/trust/market data across 40+ countries, sourcing News media (#9). The UK Gambling Commission publishes gross gambling yield and participation statistics, sourcing Gambling & betting entertainment (#20) — UK-market data specifically, not global. Gaming, social media, advertising, sports, live events, pay-TV, podcasting, esports, influencer economy, toys/merch, radio, out-of-home, and animation/VFX have no verified institutional tracker and stay editorial.
