11 of 20 items are institutionally tracked. Semiconductor sub-items — Semiconductors & chips (1), Memory chips (8), Semiconductor foundries (9), EDA software (15), and Semiconductor fabrication equipment (19) — remain backed by WSTS/SIA. The International Federation of Robotics' World Robotics report sources Robotics & automation (16). The ITU Global Cybersecurity Index (reused from Defense & security) sources Cybersecurity (11). World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade HS 8517 trade-flow data sources Smartphones (6), Telecom equipment (12), and Networking equipment (18). ITU's ICT statistics on broadband subscriptions and network coverage source Internet infrastructure (13) — connectivity/access indicators, narrower than physical infrastructure. Cloud computing, Software, Artificial intelligence, Consumer electronics, Data centers, Enterprise IT, SaaS, Quantum computing, and Wearables & IoT devices remain editorial — no institutional tracker was found for any of them (OECD Digital Economy Outlook, IEA data-centre reports, UNCTAD Digital Economy Report, and ITU DataHub IoT indicators were checked but returned bot-blocked or inconclusive).
