Institutionally tracked Editorial judgment

No single body ranks these 20 together; each item's inclusion is grounded in real data from the sources below, but the overall list and order is editorial. Items 1–16 are directly backed by SIPRI's tracked databases (arms transfers, Top 100 producers, military expenditure, nuclear forces, peace operations). Cybersecurity & cyber warfare (17) is sourced to the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index (GCI), which ranks countries' cybersecurity commitment across five pillars. Private security & military contractors (19) is sourced to the Montreux Document Forum (intergovernmental initiative, secretariat hosted by DCAF), which tracks state and international-organization participation in governance of private military and security companies. Intelligence services (18) and Border & homeland security (20) have no institutional comparative tracker and remain editorial.
Item citation: SIPRI (Arms Industry, Arms Transfers, Top 100, Military Expenditure Databases) · ITU Global Cybersecurity Index · Montreux Document Forum
