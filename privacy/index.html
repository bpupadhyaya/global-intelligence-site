Privacy Policy

The short version: we don't collect your name or create an account for you. The apps show ads, and this page explains what that involves, in plain language.

Last updated 2026-10-31

What we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. Global Intelligence — the iOS app, the Android app, and this website — does not have user accounts and does not run analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. This website uses no advertising or tracking. The apps show advertising served by Google AdMob, which receives limited device information as described in the Advertising section below. What you read, pin, search for or hide is never sent to us.

How the apps work

The only network activity the apps perform is downloading the latest briefing pack — the same public JSON data published on this site — on a schedule or when you tap refresh. That request carries no identifying information beyond what any HTTP request inherently includes (such as your IP address, visible to our hosting provider, never logged or linked to anything else). Nothing you do inside the app — what you read, pin, search for, or hide — is ever sent anywhere. It is written only to storage local to your device (UserDefaults on iOS, local preferences/storage on Android) and never leaves it.

Advertising

The free version of the apps shows advertising served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS or the Advertising ID on Android) and device and usage information such as your IP address, device type and how you interact with ads.

Where required (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads. On iOS the app also asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used for tracking or personalised ads. You can decline tracking or personalisation, reset your advertising ID, or opt out in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

A one-time Remove ads purchase removes ads. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Learn more in How Google uses data in advertising.

Notifications

Reminder notifications, when you enable them, are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. There is no push-notification server and no device token is ever collected — we have no way to send you anything remotely, even if we wanted to.

Accounts and payment

Global Intelligence has no account system of its own. The Briefing feed, Top 10, and Supply Chain are unlocked by Global Intelligence Premium, an auto-renewing monthly subscription (price shown at checkout in your store's local currency), billed and managed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play — your payment method and identity are held by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, never by us. The app receives only entitlement status (subscribed or not, ads removed or not), never payment details.

Where the briefing content comes from

The briefing is assembled from public RSS feeds published by the news outlets listed in our source registry. We summarize reporting in our own words and always link back to the original article. This is a one-way process — reading the news does not involve reading anything about you.

Children's privacy

The apps are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are handled by Google AdMob as described in the Advertising section.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@equalinformation.com, or raised as an issue on GitHub.
