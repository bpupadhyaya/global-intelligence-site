By country

Combined gold + silver + copper reserve value, by country

Not a measure of GDP or the size of a country's economy — this is specifically the value of gold, silver, and copper reserves held in the ground in each country, at current spot prices, combined into one bubble. Chile ranks #1 because it holds the world's largest copper reserves (~180 million tonnes, USGS) — its actual GDP is roughly 1% of the United States'. Items without a published per-country breakdown (Supply Chain, Banana) aren't on this map — see "By category" below for those.
